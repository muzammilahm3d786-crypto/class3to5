HIS JOURNEY

Dream. Think.
Rise.

Born on 9 November 1877 in Sialkot, British India, Allama Muhammad Iqbal became one of the most influential poets and thinkers of the Muslim world. His philosophy of Khudi encouraged people to discover their inner strength, build character and rise above limitations. Today, his words continue to inspire millions.
